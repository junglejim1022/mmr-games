Privacy Policy for Chameleon Jumper

Last updated 16 October 2026

Chameleon Jumper is a game made by MR Games. This policy explains, in plain language, what the game does and does not do with information. It applies to the iOS and Android versions.

The short version. We do not run a server and we do not have accounts. Your username, score and purchases stay on your own device, and we never see them. There are two exceptions, both described below: the advertising that funds the free version is served by Google AdMob, and if you claim the mystery gift you send us your address by email.

What stays on your device

The following is saved only in the game's own storage on your phone. It is never sent to us, and we cannot read it:

  • The username you choose
  • Your score and best score
  • Items you have unlocked and which ones you are wearing
  • Your mosquito balance
  • Sound and game settings

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect device identifiers, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with the ads. We never see this data ourselves; it goes to Google.

On iOS, the game asks for your permission before any tracking identifier is used. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised. You can change that choice at any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

What Google does with the data is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Buying "Remove Ads" turns the advertising off, and with it the data collection described in this section.

Purchases

Mosquito packs and "Remove Ads" are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. Payment details go to Apple or Google — never to us. We receive only anonymous sales totals, with no information about who bought what.

Time checks

The daily reward needs to know the real date, so that changing your phone's clock does not unlock it early. To check this, the game contacts one of these public services:

  • worldtimeapi.org
  • timeapi.io
  • cloudflare.com

As with any request to any website, those servers can see your IP address. The game sends nothing else to them, and the answer it gets back is only the current time.

The mystery gift

Reaching 1500 points in a single run wins a physical mystery gift. Claiming it is entirely voluntary, and it is the one part of the game where we do receive personal information about you.

To claim, you email us yourself. In that email you send:

  • Your name and full postal address, so the gift can be shipped
  • Your username and the verification code the game showed you
  • A screenshot of the winning screen

We use this only to verify the win and post the gift. The gift itself is free; you pay the shipping, and we tell you the exact cost before anything is sent. Shipping is paid through Swish, PayPal or Google Pay, and those services handle the payment themselves — we never see your card or bank details.

We keep your address only as long as it takes to send the gift and handle any delivery problem, and no longer than twelve months. We never sell it, and we never pass it to anyone except the postal carrier that delivers the parcel. If you want it erased sooner, email us and we will delete it.

If you never claim the gift, none of this applies and we hold nothing about you.

Children

Chameleon Jumper is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The game has no chat, no social features and no way to share personal details with other players.

Your rights

To remove everything the game has stored on your phone, delete the app.

If you have claimed the mystery gift, you may ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase it. Email mmr_games@outlook.com and we will answer within 30 days. Under the GDPR you may also complain to the Swedish authority IMY.

For the data Google collects through advertising, use Google's own ad settings.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting something new, this page is updated before that version is released, and the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the game, go to mmr_games@outlook.com.
